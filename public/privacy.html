Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 September 2026

My Wealth Square is an educational personal wealth planner, net-worth calculator and retirement simulator developed by LBP. This policy explains how the website and Android app handle information.

Information you enter

No account is required. Asset, liability, expense, retirement-planning, language and currency figures are saved locally on your device. The developer does not collect, transmit, share or sell those figures.

Retirement simulation

The retirement simulator uses assumptions chosen by you to produce an educational projection. Its calculations run on your device and are not personalised financial advice, a guarantee, or an offer of a financial product.

PDF export and sharing

Reports are generated on your device. A report leaves your device only when you choose a save or share destination. The privacy practices of that destination then apply.

Android app and website hosting

The Android app packages the calculator files on the device and does not need internet access for calculations. The separate public website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process standard technical request data to deliver and secure the website under its own privacy terms. Figures entered into My Wealth Square are not sent to GitHub by the calculator.

Advertising, analytics and third parties

My Wealth Square contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, financial-account connection or user-tracking service. It does not offer loans, banking, trading, investment, insurance or payment services.

Data retention and deletion

Locally saved figures remain until you clear the app or browser data, reset your device, or uninstall the Android app. You control any PDF copies you save or share.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the V2 support page. Do not include personal financial figures in a public support request.
